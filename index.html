About me

Who I am

I'm a CTO who still reads pull requests. Over 15+ years I've gone from web developer to architect to running technology for two companies, and I've kept one rule: own the whole stack, from the Terraform module to the on-call rotation to the roadmap conversation with the board.

I've taken companies from legacy monoliths to cloud-native microservices on AWS, and I've done the unglamorous work that makes that stick: CI/CD, observability, cost control, security, and technical due diligence with investors.

Today I'm focused on AI systems in production: autonomous agents, RAG pipelines, LLM orchestration and MCP tooling, built with the same discipline as any other backend service. I speak Spanish and English and work equally well in either.
